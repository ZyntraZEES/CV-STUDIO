Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who we are

CV Studio ("the app") is a resume builder for Android published by Global Nova Technologies ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

In short: your resumes are stored on your phone. Signing in with Google is optional and only used to back up and restore your resumes. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Everything you type into the app (your name, contact details, work history, education, skills, profile details and preferences such as dark mode) is saved locally on your phone. It stays there unless you choose to sign in, export a PDF, or share it yourself.

Optional Google sign-in and cloud backup

If you choose Continue with Google, we use Google Firebase to:

  • Sign you in (Firebase Authentication). We receive your Google account name, email address and a unique user ID.
  • Back up your data (Cloud Firestore). Your resumes and profile details are stored in a private record linked to your user ID, so you can restore them on another phone.

Only your signed-in account can read or write your backup. Firebase is operated by Google LLC; see Firebase's privacy information.

Fonts

The app downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts the first time it runs. This request shares your device's IP address with Google, as with any web request. No personal content is sent.

PDF export and sharing

PDFs are created on your phone. When you share or print one, it goes only to the app or printer you choose.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash-tracking or behavioural tracking.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, location or files.

How long we keep data

Local data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Cloud backups are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account. Sign-in is never required.
  • Sign out any time from Profile → Account & backup.
  • Delete your account and backup from Profile → Account & backup → Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Delete local resumes from Profile → Privacy & data → Delete all resumes.

Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. Access to each backup is restricted to the account that owns it.

Children

CV Studio is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
