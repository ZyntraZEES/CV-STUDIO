Delete your CV Studio account

CV Studio by Global Nova Technologies

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open CV Studio and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Account & backup, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and cloud backup are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, email ahmiikhanx885@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "CV Studio account deletion". We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your CV Studio sign-in account (Google name, email and user ID).
  • Your cloud backup: all resumes and profile details stored in Firebase.
Resumes saved on your phone are not affected by account deletion. To remove them too, use Profile → Privacy & data → Delete all resumes, or uninstall the app. We do not keep any copies after deletion.

Don't have an account?

If you never signed in with Google, CV Studio has no data about you on our servers. Everything is on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it.
